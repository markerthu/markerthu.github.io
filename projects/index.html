Preprint 2026

Vanguard: Knowing When to Trust the Draft in Speculative Vision-Language Agents

H. Mao*, J. Fan*, R. Guo, S. Shen, Z. Wang, H. Ren, X. Zhong, G. Liu

A small draft that leads its large target

Let a small model go first: a decision model checks each drafted step of an agent against the screen, keeps it when confident and calls the large model only when needed. Training-free, across model families, and more accurate than the large model alone on web and household tasks.

Preprint 2026

Language Models Can Rewrite Their Own Weights

J. Fan*, N. He, C. Yang, A. Mao, X. Zhu, C. Cheng, K. Liang, S. Shen, Z. Li, R. Guo, G. Liu

A third way to make a model better

For the first time, to our knowledge, a language model rewrites its own weights by its own reasoning and becomes better, with no gradients and no training: the model is its own optimizer. It surpasses itself on problems it never saw, keeps getting better session after session, and repairs a damaged copy of itself without ever seeing its original weights.

AudioRSI: Recursively Self-Improving Audio Reasoners

Surpasses its teacher, then becomes it
