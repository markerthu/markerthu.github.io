At a glance

The paper in four results.

Reinforcement learning fine-tuning keeps a generative model near where it started with one fixed penalty, the same for every sample. What if each sample set its own? ADRPO loosens it for samples better than their batch and tightens it for worse ones.

The problem One fixed strength

Fine-tuning holds every sample back equally hard. That forces a trade.

Strong regularization keeps what the model could do but limits how far the reward can rise. Weak regularization lets the reward rise but risks collapse and reward hacking. PPO, GRPO and DPO all pick one strength and use it for every sample.

{% include proj/adrpo/dilemma.html %}

A good sample and a bad one are held back exactly the same.

The idea Adaptive regularization

Let each sample set its own strength: β0 − A.

A is the sample’s advantage: how much its reward beats the average of its batch. The model may move further toward good samples, and stays close to where it started on poor ones.

One coefficient, set per sample

The advantage is the signal the update already uses. ADRPO reads it a second time to set the regularization.

  • Better than its batch, A > 0Weaker regularization: the model can exploit it.
  • Worse than its batch, A < 0Stronger regularization: the model stays near the original.
  • ClippedA is kept within a fixed range for stable training.

Exploit where the samples are good, stay careful where they are not.

How it works

One coefficient changes, in the loss you already use.

In GRPO, only the fixed β changes: it becomes β0 − A. On flow models, the reward weight also becomes the advantage.

Flow models, with W2

Online fine-tuning of SD3; the divergence compares its velocity field with the pre-trained one.

Fixed: ORW-CFM-W2L = LORW + β · ‖vθ − vref‖2

ADRPOL = LRL + (β0 − A) · ‖vθ − vref‖2

LRL weights each sample by its advantage, so poor samples are pushed away rather than only weighted down.

Language and audio models, with KL

GRPO fine-tuning of an LLM or a multimodal reasoning model; the divergence is the KL to the reference model.

Fixed: GRPOL = LPG + β · DKL(πθ ‖ πref)

ADRPOL = LPG + (β0 − A) · DKL(πθ ‖ πref)

A is GRPO’s own group advantage; β0 equals the fixed β it replaces.

  • no new networks
  • no architectural changes
  • no manual schedule

The evidence Finding 01

A 2B model beats models up to six times its size.

Fine-tuned with ADRPO, SD3 (2B) scores above SANA-1.5 (4.8B) and FLUX.1-Dev (12B) on all six metrics, and keeps the styles, colors and layouts the prompts ask for.

The evidence Finding 03

On a language model, it escapes a local optimum by itself.

Plugged into GRPO to fine-tune Qwen3 (0.6B), ADRPO first narrows its outputs, then widens them again to climb out. GRPO stays at high entropy and struggles to find higher reward.

The evidence Finding 04

A 7B audio model beats Gemini 2.5 Pro and GPT-4o Audio.

Fine-tuned on AVQA and tested on MMAU, Qwen2.5-Omni-7B with ADRPO answers more audio questions right than with GRPO, and more than both commercial models.

Temporal sequenceWhat was the last sound in the sequence?footsteps, dog barking, camera shutter clicking, tapping on glass

GRPOThe last sound in the sequence is a camera shutter click, which is a distinct sound from the other options provided.Camera shutter clicking, wrong
ADRPO… The last sound is a distinct ‘tapping on glass’, which is a clear and unique sound compared to the others.Tapping on glass, correct

Source identificationBased on the given audio, identify the source of the flowing water.Bathtub, River, Fountain, Rain

GRPOThe sound is consistent with a steady flow of water, likely from a man-made source like a bathtub or fountain rather than natural sources like a river or rain.Fountain, wrong
ADRPOThe audio suggests a continuous, steady flow of water, which is characteristic of a bathtub. …Bathtub, correct

Contextual reasoningConsidering the information in the audio, what type of weather condition can be inferred?Windy, Calm, Rainy, Snowy

GRPOThe presence of wind noise and the sound of rain hitting a surface suggest a windy and rainy weather condition.Rainy, wrong
ADRPOThe presence of wind noise suggests a windy condition. The absence of rain or snow sounds indicates it’s not rainy or snowy.Windy, correct

Why it matters

One rule for images, text and audio.

ADRPO plugs into the reinforcement learning fine-tuning people already run, for continuous and discrete models alike.

Images

Flow matching on SD3, with W2 regularization.

beats 4.8B and 12B models

Text

GRPO on Qwen2 and Qwen3, with KL regularization.

escapes a local optimum

Audio reasoning

GRPO on Qwen2.5-Omni-7B, with KL regularization.

beats Gemini 2.5 Pro

What this opens up

Smaller models that compete

A small model fine-tuned well can stand with much larger ones, and it costs less to run.

Exploration when it pays

The advantage tells the model when to stay close and when to move on, so it can climb out of a local optimum.

A drop-in change

One coefficient becomes a function of the advantage. In GRPO, nothing else changes.
