At a glance

The paper in four results.

Why do audio language models get worse the longer they think? Their training never rewards how they reason. Reward the reasoning itself, and thinking turns from a liability into a gain.

The problem

Audio language models get worse the longer they think.

Chain-of-thought helps language models on text. In audio it backfires: the longer the base model reasons, the lower its accuracy, and a model trained with outcome-only RL gains nothing clear from reasoning.

  • made-up facts
  • flawed logic
  • answers that contradict the reasoning
  • reasoning that loops

The paper’s test-time scaling sweep: the limit on thinking length is raised step by step, and each point is placed at the average length the model actually used.

The culprit is not reasoning itself. It is training that never looks at the reasoning.

The idea From outcomes to process

Reward the reasoning, not only the answer.

Outcome-only RL cannot tell a sound chain of reasoning from a lucky one. CESAR scores how the model got to its answer, so sound reasoning is what gets reinforced.

{% include proj/cesar/idea.html %}

Reasoning stops being a side effect and becomes a skill the model is trained for.

How it works

Two rewards check the outcome. Three shape the reasoning.

For each training question the model writes eight answers. Each gets one score, the weighted sum of five terms, and GRPO reinforces the answers that beat the group’s average.

What each part adds

MMAU Test-mini. The paper’s progressive ablation, read from the base model up; every model reasons, at its own sweet spot.

The three reasoning terms target the ways reasoning fails: drifting from the question, contradicting its own answer, wandering without structure, overthinking.

The evidence Finding 01

Reasoning becomes a gain.

Turn reasoning on, and the base model loses accuracy while CESAR gains it. Give it more room to think, and its accuracy peaks at a sweet spot.

MMAU Test-mini, total accuracy. The sweep raises the thinking-length limit from 0 to 250 in steps of 25; the second chart shows each model without reasoning and with reasoning at its best length.

A model must first learn to think well before thinking more pays off.

The evidence Finding 02

A new state of the art on MMAU.

With 7B parameters, CESAR is ahead of Gemini 2.5 Pro, Gemini 2.5 Flash and GPT‑4o Audio on MMAU Test-mini, a benchmark that shares nothing with its training data.

Qwen2.5-Omni-7B and Ke-Omni-R were run by the paper under the same protocol, each shown in its better mode; the other scores come from the MMAU paper. CESAR also has the best Sound and Music scores in the paper’s table.

The gain comes from scaling the reasoning, not the parameter count.

The evidence Finding 03

Its reasoning is better, not just its answers.

Asked which of two reasoning traces is more logical and more faithful to the audio, a GPT‑4o Audio judge picks CESAR’s most of the time.

MMAU Test-mini. The judge sees the audio, the question, the choices, the correct answer and both traces; ties are split evenly between the two.

Its answers follow from its reasoning, and its reasoning follows the audio.

The evidence Finding 04

It listens better, too.

Trained to reason well, it also answers better than its base model when it does not reason at all. On MMSU it has the best perception and reasoning averages of any model, and passes humans on semantic reasoning.

MMSU separates perception tasks from reasoning tasks. Ke-Omni-R is the paper’s own run; the other MMSU scores come from the MMSU paper.

Better thinking, it turns out, leads to better hearing.

Why it matters Finding 05

Reasoning no longer has to be a matter of chance. It can be a matter of design.

One reward term changes how the model reasons. Without the overthinking penalty it becomes a depth specialist that does best with long chains on hard questions; with it, a calibrated generalist that peaks with short ones and gains more from reasoning.

MMAU Test-mini, from the same sweep as Finding 01. Dashed: each model without reasoning.

What this opens up

Train the reasoning, then set its budget

First teach the model to reason well. Then sweep its thinking length at test time to find the sweet spot, with no further training.

Small models that reason

A 7B model ahead of proprietary systems, by scaling how it reasons rather than how big it is.

A measure of reasoning itself

A judge that compares reasoning traces head to head shows what accuracy alone cannot.
