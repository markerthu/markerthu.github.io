At a glance

The paper in four results.

What does an audio model work out before it speaks? Read with a logit lens while it listens, the answer to a spoken question becomes legible, in words, in its middle layers, before it emits a single token.

The problem

We see what it says, never what it worked out.

Chain-of-thought monitoring helps only if a model writes its reasoning down. An audio model that answers a spoken question directly writes nothing, and its own transcript of the sound can fail.

The idea Read the middle

A chain-of-thought monitor reads what a model writes. We read what it works out.

No chain-of-thought needed: the model’s own output head turns its middle layers into words.

How it works

A lens on the sound, and a control that changes only the sound.

The logit lens decodes a middle layer with the model’s own output head, at the audio positions. The audio comes before the question, so those positions cannot see the text. To rule out a guess from the printed options, the text stays byte-identical and only the waveform changes.

  • no chain-of-thought
  • no training
  • no trained probe

Schematic. The model is the 48-layer Thinker of Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Instruct; the middle band, layers 17 to 38 (about 35 to 80% of depth), is fixed in advance from work on text models, not tuned on these data.

If the readout follows the sound, it is the sound the model is thinking about.

The evidence Finding 03

Over the 1000-clip MMAU-mini, answering from the sound beats answering from its caption on every track: by 7.6 points overall and 11.1 on the sound track.

The concept the answer depends on forms in one mind and not in the other.

The evidence Finding 04

It lives in the middle, and the model uses it.

Swap only the sound and the readout follows it. The gap over the text prior opens a tenth of the way in and stays open; patch the sound back in and the answer returns.

Left: real clip minus silence, per depth; band: 95% bootstrap interval; filled: significant (paired McNemar). Right, below: copying the real-audio states back into a run whose audio was zeroed. The same signature holds on Qwen2.5-Omni-7B.

What deleting one layer does

Bars: accuracy drop when each layer is replaced by an identity skip, over 40 clips. Lanes: which ability one deleted layer breaks, on the paper’s Kennedy clip.

The sound’s content is used, and committed before the last fifth of the network.

Why it matters Finding 05

A hallucination is born early, where it can be seen.

Fed its own caption instead of the sound, the model sometimes settles on a plausible wrong answer. Reading its layers shows where: usually early, and never revised.

Each dot is one clip, in the band of layers where the wrong concept first takes over the run fed its caption. 84.9% of the wrong answers form before the output layer. Reading both minds side by side detects the hallucination, since the audio mind holds the right concept where the text mind holds the wrong one, and shows where it entered.

What this opens up

Oversight before it acts

If a speech agent’s decisions are legible this way, a monitor need not wait for it to speak, or trust it to narrate.

A signal for training

A readout of the middle layers is a target for the process, where a scalar reward sees only the final answer.

Almost free to run

A logit-lens probe costs one matrix product per position read.

In short

Before an audio model speaks, its answer is already there, in words, in its middle layers.

It never wrote its reasoning down. We read it anyway.
