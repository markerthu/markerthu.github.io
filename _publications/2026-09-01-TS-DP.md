Speculative decoding for diffusion policies. Diffusion policies in embodied control are I/O-bound rather than compute-bound, so TS-DP distills a Transformer drafter that imitates the base policy's costly denoising calls, and an RL scheduler adjusts the speculative parameters as task difficulty changes over time. In simulation it cuts function evaluations by up to 4.17× and wall-clock time by up to 3.2× with a draft acceptance rate above 85%; on real robots it cuts evaluations 3.8× and runs at 26 Hz, raising success on real-time tasks such as a conveyor belt.
