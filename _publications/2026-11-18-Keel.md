The first agentic weight-editing method for vision-language models on image tasks: told only the symptom, a frontier multimodal LLM acting as an editor locates why small reasoning VLMs fall into loops in thinking mode, edits the weights and tests them, and the harness keeps only edits that reduce the failure without lowering accuracy on unseen questions. The repair needs no training and keeps the model's size and per-token cost. On Qwen3.5-0.8B and Qwen3.5-2B across six image benchmarks, from visual mathematics to GUI grounding, the edit removes most loops on every benchmark and the edited models are significantly more accurate than the thinking models on average.
