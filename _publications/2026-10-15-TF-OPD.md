Specialists sharing a base model merge into one with forward passes only: each linear layer is fit in closed form to every specialist's outputs on its own answers. One forward pass over a few hundred rollouts provides the statistics, and what those rollouts contain matters more than the solver. The fused model is, on average, more accurate than either specialist alone.
