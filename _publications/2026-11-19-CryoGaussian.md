A Gaussian splatting method for heterogeneous cryo-EM reconstruction that fits an explicit Gaussian density directly to particle images through a differentiable rasterizer, with learned particle-image and per-Gaussian latents that modulate each Gaussian's position, amplitude and scale. On CryoBench it achieves state-of-the-art reconstruction accuracy for both compositional and conformational heterogeneity; on experimental data it resolves a rare 50S ribosomal state (1.3% of particles), and the optimized rasterizer cuts training time by 12.8-28.0×.
