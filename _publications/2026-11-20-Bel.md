A visual agent's right action is often among its own samples, passed over by its greedy decode. Bel trains nothing: it draws each candidate action on its own copy of the image it acts on, asks a decision model for the probability that executing it is the right decision, and acts on another candidate only when greedy scores below a threshold and trails it by a margin. For each of three policies it significantly beats greedy decoding on mobile, web and desktop screens, and it significantly raises live AndroidWorld and MiniWoB++ task success and robot-pointing success.
