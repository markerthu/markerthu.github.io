M3 is a training-free, multi-agent loop for compositional text-to-image generation: a Planner turns the prompt into a verifiable checklist, Checker, Refiner and Editor agents fix one constraint at a time, and a Verifier keeps every round an improvement. Applied to open-source models, Qwen-Image with M3 reaches state of the art on OneIG-EN, ahead of commercial systems such as Imagen 4 and Seedream 3.0.
