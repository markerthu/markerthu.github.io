SELF-POT is a benchmark and evaluation framework for how much a model gains from test-time computation, across competition mathematics, competitive programming and agentic workflows. Under one budget rule that charges every model call, including selection and critique, in dollars, it compares direct inference with parallel sampling and self-revision, and asks when a cheaper model with more inference can match a stronger one.
