DM-PRM turns a general-purpose decision model, one that answers yes/no questions with probabilities, into a general-purpose process reward model zero-shot: no step labels, no fine-tuning, no critic. It asks one question per step, adds the standardised answers to the advantage, and plugs into any policy-gradient method. With Qwen2.5-1.5B it lifts GRPO by over twenty points on ALFWorld and over ten on WebShop, and it also improves GiGPO and GraphGPO on both benchmarks.
