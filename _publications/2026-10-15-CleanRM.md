CleanRM trains reward models from noisy preference feedback with the help of a few clean labels: optimal transport calibrates a noise ratio for each sample, and a noise-corrected surrogate loss makes the objective unbiased with respect to the clean risk. It beats denoising baselines and gives better downstream RLHF alignment.
